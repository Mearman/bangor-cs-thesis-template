\section{Float numbering}
The class numbers floats by section by default. Figures, tables, and listings share one counter, and the counter restarts in each section, so \cref{tab:numbering} is the first float of this section and its number says so, while \cref{tab:sample} and \cref{fig:crest} belong to the previous section. A float that comes before the first section of a chapter is numbered by the chapter alone, and \texttt{sectionfloats=zero} numbers it by section zero instead. Set \texttt{sharedfloats=false} or \texttt{sectionfloats=none} in the class options to turn either behaviour off.
